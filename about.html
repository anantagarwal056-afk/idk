About National Techno Kraft

Established in 2002, National Techno Kraft is a trusted manufacturer of corrugated cartons, printed boxes and customized packaging solutions serving businesses across India.

Who We Are

We specialize in manufacturing high-quality corrugated packaging for pesticide, automotive, oil, footwear, retail and industrial sectors. Every box is crafted using premium kraft materials and modern machinery to ensure durability and precision.

What We Do

From standard shipping cartons to fully customized printed and laminated boxes, we provide packaging solutions tailored to your product size, weight and branding requirements.

Our Experience

With over two decades of industry experience, we have built long-term partnerships with leading brands by delivering consistent quality, on-time production and dependable service.

Our Manufacturing Approach

We combine skilled manpower with advanced machinery to maintain strict quality control at every stage of production.

Modern Machinery

Equipped with corrugation machines, sheet cutters, slotting machines, die-cut punchers and automatic pasting systems to deliver consistent output.

Quality Assurance

Every product goes through multiple inspections ensuring strength, finishing and dimensional accuracy before dispatch.

Custom Packaging

We manufacture packaging in various sizes, ply options and designs based on client requirements with fast turnaround time.
